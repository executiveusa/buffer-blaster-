# NO IDOL NO RIVAL, v2: "The Idols Fall"

Modern version. No fisheye, no chrome type, no strobes. One big idea, shot like a 2025 high-budget video:
smooth motion-control camera moves, anamorphic look (shallow focus, oval bokeh, horizontal flares),
one hard gold top light against black, real haze, macro texture shots, and cuts on the beat.

**Idea:** Jalen walks alone across a huge black stage lined with giant marble "idol" statues.
Each one cracks and collapses as he passes. He never looks back. No idol. No rival.
The statues are faceless (smooth marble heads) and wear symbols of hype: crowns, chains, trophies.
That keeps them anonymous, so no real person's likeness is used, and lets anyone read their own idols into them.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, about 20 s, 1080x1920. Audience 16 to 30.
**Music:** original dark cinematic trap, half-time ~70 BPM (140 double-time hats), heavy 808, low choir pad,
silence right before the first statue breaks, then the drop. Made on Fal ACE-Step.
**Type:** clean, modern condensed sans, small, wide-tracked, off-white and gold. Motion Master renders it.

## Why these models for the statues
- **Veo 3.1** has the most believable physics on Fal right now for breaking solid objects (weight, chunks, dust),
  and it makes matching sound (stone cracking, rubble hitting the floor), which sells the realism.
- Each statue shot starts from a still frame we approve first (Nano Banana Pro, $0.15), so the stage and statues
  look right before paying for motion.
- AI video still fails sometimes on destruction, so the plan keeps money for one retake of each statue shot.
