## Edit style (late-90s hip-hop video look)
Fisheye barrel warp on the AI shots, crushed blacks and high contrast, saturated color, glossy highlights,
white flash frames on every bar, 2 to 4 frame strobes, speed ramps (slow-mo into a hard cut), slight RGB split on hits,
film grain. Cuts land on kicks and snares. Style reference only: no real artist's music, face, voice or logo.

## Music
Original boom-bap beat (~95 BPM, dusty drums, deep bass, piano/string stab), instrumental, generated on
Fal ACE-Step (Apache-2.0 model, $0.0002 per second, so a 30 s take is about $0.01). Hits, whooshes and risers
from Motion Master's sound library (earshot MIT, uisfx CC0).
