# Look book: late-90s hip-hop video (style reference only)

What defines the look, and how this promo uses it. No real artist's music, face, voice, logos or footage.

| Trait | How we use it |
|---|---|
| Fisheye lens, very close to the subject, low angle | Barrel warp on every Jalen shot; prompts ask for "fisheye lens, low angle, close" |
| Doubled frame rate / slow motion that stays intimate | Generate clips with slow movement, play some at 0.5x, hard-cut out on the snare |
| Chrome and glossy surfaces | Chrome-gradient title type; prompts ask for wet streets, glossy puffer sheen |
| Strobe lights, white flash frames | 1 to 2 white frames on bar starts; strobe through the colorways |
| Saturated single-color washes (electric blue, red, gold) and neon | Intro backgrounds pulse blue, red, gold; grade pushes saturation and crushes blacks |
| Widescreen letterbox inside the frame | Black bars slide in on the hero shots |
| Futuristic, larger than life, but the subject owns the frame | Jalen centered, looking down the lens, minimal background |
