# Buffer Blaster Positioning

## Category
One-time private AI ad factory install for client teams and AI agents.

## Primary job
Turn product truth, customer signals, and brand context into testable ad creative while keeping model choice, approvals, cost controls, and learning evidence in one owned system.

## Commercial model
Buffer Blaster is not a public SaaS subscription.

The primary offer is:

**Private Install** — a one-time implementation of Buffer Blaster in infrastructure controlled by the client/operator, with provider-neutral generation, Studio access, REST/MCP/CLI, approval controls, documentation, and rollback.

Ongoing cloud, model, API, and third-party provider usage are separate from the Buffer Blaster installation fee and remain in accounts controlled by the operator.

Managed implementation/support may be sold separately when needed, but it is not the public subscription model.

## What makes it different
- The customer owns the deployment instead of renting another creative SaaS seat.
- Research, creative planning, generation routing, approvals, output, and evidence are connected.
- Multiple configured generation gateways can coexist, so the workflow is not tied to one video-model vendor.
- People and agents enter through the same governed backend.
- Paid generation, publishing, and ad activation stay human-controlled.
- The production ledger can be sovereign/self-hosted.
- Provider credentials remain operator-controlled.

## Public language
Prefer: AI ad factory, one-time private install, own the system, your infrastructure, your model accounts, angle, creative, UGC-style ad, review, approval, cost limit, evidence, private install.

Avoid on public pages unless explaining developer access: canonical ledger, configured media boundary, service role, deterministic receipt, gate, orchestration, RLS, PostgREST.

## Claim discipline
Do not claim guaranteed ROAS, conversion lift, automatic winning ads, active provider connections, or unlimited model access without runtime evidence. "Provider-neutral" means the install can route configured and allowlisted providers/models; it does not mean every model is automatically available.

## Benchmark logic
Arcads, MakeUGC, and MaxFusion are product-clarity and proof-density references, not visual templates. Buffer Blaster should match their immediate category clarity while differentiating on ownership: one-time private installation, operator-controlled providers, agent access, and governed execution.

The landing page order is:
**Outcome → real output → workflow → model routing → install offer.**
