# Buffer Blaster Positioning

## Category
Private creative infrastructure for client teams and AI agents.

## Primary job
Turn product truth, customer signals, and brand context into testable ad creative, while keeping approvals, cost controls, and learning evidence in one governed system.

## Commercial model
Buffer Blaster is not positioned as a low-ticket public subscription. The software is used in two ways:

1. **Managed Creative Engine** — we operate it inside a client engagement and sell the outcome.
2. **Private Install** — a dedicated deployment for teams that need their own infrastructure and agent access.

## What makes it different
- Research/creative/approval/evidence are connected instead of scattered across token tools.
- People and agents enter through the same governed backend.
- Paid generation, publishing, and ad activation stay human-controlled.
- The production ledger can be sovereign/self-hosted.
- Provider connections are client-scoped rather than assumed globally.

## Public language
Prefer: angle, creative, UGC-style ad, review, approval, cost limit, evidence, client workspace, private install, managed system.

Avoid on public pages unless explaining developer access: canonical ledger, configured media boundary, service role, deterministic receipt, gate, orchestration, RLS, PostgREST.

## Claim discipline
Do not claim guaranteed ROAS, conversion lift, automatic winning ads, active Meta/TikTok delivery, or active Shopify account access without current runtime evidence. "Learn what works" means the system retains real evidence when traffic/provider data exists; it does not invent performance.

## Benchmark logic
MaxFusion is the product-clarity bar: creative infrastructure, end-to-end flow, agent/MCP access. We do not copy its visual identity or compete on mass-market seat pricing. Our defensible difference is private client infrastructure, governed execution, sovereign deployment, and integration into the client's operating system.
