# Stage 02 — Create

Job: turn the approved campaign brief into editable social and UGC creative artifacts.

## Reads

Working: `../01_brief/output/brief.md`.
Reference: `../../../_system/CONTEXT.md`, repository UGC skill, and campaign-linked moodboard/product references.

## Process

Create only the formats required by the brief. For UGC, compile prompts in the universal order scene → camera → subject → environment → lighting/style → motion → optional dialogue. Generate variants, score them with the repository rubric, and preserve render request/response IDs when media is submitted.

## Writes

- `output/content.md` — canonical copy/format plan
- `output/ugc-prompts.md` — provider-neutral UGC prompts when needed
- `output/render-receipts.md` — render job IDs/URLs/status pointers when submitted

## Human check

A human reviews factual accuracy, brand fit, product representation, and the shortlisted creative before anything becomes approved for publishing.
