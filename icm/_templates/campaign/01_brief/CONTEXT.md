# Stage 01 — Brief

Job: turn the requested business outcome into one canonical campaign brief.

## Reads

Working: `input/request.md` if present.
Reference: `../../../_system/CONTEXT.md`.

## Process

Capture only facts needed to run the campaign: brand, objective, audience, offer, dates/duration, platforms, mandatory product references, and constraints. Flag unknowns rather than inventing them.

## Writes

`output/brief.md` — canonical campaign brief, one home for campaign facts.

## Human check

A human confirms the objective/offer and any externally visible factual claims before creation proceeds.
