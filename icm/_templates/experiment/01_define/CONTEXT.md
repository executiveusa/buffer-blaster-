# 01_define — define the measurable test

One job: turn the Hermes opportunity/proof hypothesis into a bounded experiment contract.

## Inputs
- Working: Hermes opportunity/proof handoff artifact.
- Reference: ../../../../_system/CONTEXT.md

Do NOT load: unrelated campaigns, prior experiments, provider credentials.

## Process
1. Record the business constraint and hypothesis.
2. Set one primary KPI, baseline when known, pass threshold, kill threshold, sample floor, attribution window, and budget ceiling.
3. Name control and intended variant roles; do not launch anything.

## Outputs
- experiment.md → output/

## Human check
Verify the KPI and thresholds match the claim being tested and the budget ceiling is acceptable. Edit in place before binding channels.
