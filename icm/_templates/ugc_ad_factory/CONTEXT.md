# UGC Ad Factory ICM

## Outcome
Turn one truthful product brief into one reviewable, billable UGC ad package without coupling the workflow to a specific media model.

## Walk
1. `01_research` — establish product mechanism, audience pain, competitor/reference evidence.
2. `02_script_gate` — produce natural two-clip dialogue and pass mechanical gates before paid calls.
3. `03_cast` — define a rights-clean creator/casting direction and stable visual identity.
4. `04_generate` — call the configured media provider for clip 1, then clip 2 from the approved seed frame.
5. `05_seam_qa` — verify continuity, trim order, seam quality, product identity, and output integrity.
6. `06_deliver` — package the finished 9:16 ad, evidence, cost receipt, and approval state.

## Rules
- Research before script.
- Gate before generation.
- Never hand a failed prompt to a paid generation call.
- Provider/model selection stays outside this ICM template.
- No publishing without explicit human approval.
- Cost and quote metadata are estimates until reconciled against actual receipts.
