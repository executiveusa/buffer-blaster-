# 01 Research

## Input
Product, audience, offer, user-supplied competitor leads or search terms.

## Required truth
- Product mechanism: the specific action/process that creates the result.
- Audience pain in concrete language.
- At least one evidence source before claims are promoted into script facts.
- Separate hooks from pitches when reviewing reference ads.

## Output
A structured brief containing `product`, `audience`, `pain`, `mechanism`, `offer`, and source notes.

## Gate
No mechanism or pain truth → stop before scripting. Metadata alone is not treated as creative research proof.
