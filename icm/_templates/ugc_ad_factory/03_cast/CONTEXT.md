# 03 Cast

## Goal
Create a rights-clean, believable creator direction whose first-frame lighting and framing can survive into both clips.

## Rules
- Use user-owned/licensed references only as visual direction; do not claim identity rights that are not established.
- Prefer ordinary, imperfect framing over studio-polished influencer imagery.
- Describe visible physiology, framing, light, background, and composition rather than inventing identity traits.
- Match the casting still's lighting to the intended scene.

## Output
Approved creator description, visual lane, reference provenance, and first-frame direction.
