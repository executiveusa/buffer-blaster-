# 06 Deliver

## Deliverable
One finished vertical UGC ad package plus evidence.

## Package
- final 9:16 asset reference
- approved scripts/prompts
- generation request IDs/receipts when available
- seam/quality QA result
- quoted price and estimated/actual production cost
- gross-margin reconciliation
- publish approval state

## Commercial rule
The billable unit is `finished_ugc_ad`. Quote metadata never charges a customer. Payment and publishing remain separate explicitly approved actions.

## Completion gate
Do not mark delivered until the final asset is reviewable and QA evidence exists. Do not mark published without explicit human approval.
