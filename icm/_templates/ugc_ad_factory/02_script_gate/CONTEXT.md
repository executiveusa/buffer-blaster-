# 02 Script Gate

## Structure
- Clip 1: problem + tension.
- Clip 2: mechanism + reluctant resolution.
- Target 18–32 spoken words per 10-second clip.

## Natural-speech rules
- The creator is sharing, not presenting ad copy.
- Avoid em dashes, polished closers, miracle claims, and direct-response commands.
- Keep the product mention smaller than the pain/mechanism story.
- End like normal speech, not a slogan.

## Gate
Use the factory service mechanical gate. A failed gate cannot proceed to paid generation.

## Output
Two approved scripts plus their compiled provider-neutral prompts.
